About Us

Our Story, Vision & Mission

Chiedza Chevanotenda Apostolic Church — For All Nations — a church born of prayer, built on the Word, and called to shine the light of Christ to every corner of the earth.

✦

The Founder & Visionary

A servant of God, a shepherd of many.

The founder of Chiedza Chevanotenda Apostolic Church is a man of God with a burning passion for souls and a clear calling to raise a Bible-believing, Spirit-filled church for all nations.

On 7 November 2021, under his leadership, the church was officially founded. From that first gathering, the vision grew — assembly after assembly, community after community — until today the church stands as a beacon of hope across Zimbabwe.

His ministry emphasises love — loving God and one another; the Gospel — preaching Christ crucified, risen and coming again; and humility — serving as Christ served us.

Our History

From a small gathering to a growing family

7 November 2021

The Church Is Founded

Chiedza Chevanotenda Apostolic Church — For All Nations — is officially founded by our visionary leader, beginning with a small, devoted gathering of believers.

2022 — 2023

Growth & New Assemblies

The church begins to expand — new assemblies are planted across Zimbabwe: Bhora, Murehwa, Musami, Zorizozo, Mukombami, Mwanza, St John (Chikwaka), Eastview, Hoply and Gejo RaRubi.

Today

A Church for All Nations

Ten assemblies and counting — united in love, faithful to the Gospel and walking in humility. Our vision remains unchanged: to shine the light of Christ to every nation, tribe and tongue.

Our Vision

To be a Spirit-filled, Bible-believing apostolic church that shines the light of Jesus Christ to all nations — raising disciples who love God, live by the Gospel and walk in humility.

Our Motto

Love · Gospel · Humility

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ to every creature, to disciple believers in the Word of God, to serve our communities with love, and to prepare a people ready for the return of our Lord.
