The Founder & Visionary

A shepherd after God's own heart.

Chiedza Chevanotenda Apostolic Church was founded on 7 November 2021 under the spiritual leadership of our founder — a man called by God to raise a church for all nations, where love, the Gospel and humility shape every believer.

What began as a small gathering of devoted souls has grown into a family of assemblies spanning Zimbabwe — from Bhora to Gejo RaRubi, from Murehwa to Mwanza — each one carrying the same vision: to be the light of Christ in every community.

“We are not called to build buildings — we are called to build people, for the Kingdom of God.” — The Founder, CCACFAN
