Our Services & Gatherings

Worship, Word & Fellowship

Every week we gather to praise God, hear His Word and strengthen one another. All are welcome — come and be part of the family.

Weekly Services

Times of worship & prayer

Our regular services across all assemblies.

⛪

Sunday Worship Service

Our main gathering of the week — praise, worship, the preaching of the Word, and Holy Communion.

  • Praise & worship
  • Sermon from the Word
  • Holy Communion
  • Children's ministry
🕊️

Midweek Prayer Service

A time of united prayer, intercession and seeking the face of God as one body.

  • Corporate prayer
  • Intercession for the nations
  • Prayer for the sick
  • Testimonies
📖

Bible Study

Digging deep into the Scriptures together — growing in knowledge, faith and understanding.

  • Verse-by-verse teaching
  • Group discussion
  • Questions & answers
  • Discipleship
🙏

Night Vigil & Fasting

Special times of prayer, fasting and seeking God — held regularly through the year.

  • All-night prayer
  • Corporate fasting
  • Deliverance ministry
  • Consecration
💍

Weddings & Holy Matrimony

Christ-centred weddings conducted with dignity, joy and the blessing of the church.

  • Pre-marital counselling
  • Church & outdoor ceremonies
  • Reception coordination
👶

Baptism & Dedication

Water baptism for believers, and the dedication of infants to the Lord.

  • Water baptism
  • Infant dedication
  • Family blessings
🕯️

Funeral & Memorial Services

Walking with families through grief — with prayer, comfort and Christian hope.

  • Funeral service
  • Burial rites
  • Memorial service
  • Family support
👥

Youth & Children's Ministry

Raising the next generation in the fear and knowledge of the Lord.

  • Sunday school
  • Youth fellowship
  • Sports & talent
  • Mentorship
